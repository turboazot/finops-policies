The planned Enable Banking integration is limited to read-only access to the owner’s own personal accounts, subject to valid bank consent and Enable Banking’s applicable terms. Business, professional or third-party-account use is outside this personal integration’s scope and requires separate eligibility and contractual arrangements where applicable.

Reports are informational personal summaries, not financial, investment or tax advice. Bank data may be delayed, incomplete or duplicated, and automatic categorisation may be incorrect. The owner should check important results against bank statements. Availability depends on the bank, Enable Banking, the local computer and valid consent; uninterrupted operation is not promised.

Use of Enable Banking and the bank remains subject to their respective agreements. These terms do not replace or modify them. Read the Finops privacy notice and Enable Banking’s terms.
