Privacy notice

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Purpose and scope

Finops is operated by its owner, GitHub user turboazot, for personal analysis of the owner’s own bank accounts. It is not open for registration by other users. This notice covers the planned Enable Banking connection and the resulting local data processing.

Information processed

With the owner’s bank consent, Finops will retrieve account identifiers, account details, currencies, balances and transaction history made available through Enable Banking. Transaction records may include dates, amounts, descriptions and counterparty names or account details. Finops also processes manually imported statements, categorisation rules and generated reports.

How the information is used

Information is used to synchronise personal financial records, categorise transactions and produce local income and expense reports. The integration is intended for account information only; Finops does not provide payment initiation.

Bank connection and third parties

Enable Banking processes bank data to deliver it to the authorised application. Bank authentication and consent take place through the provider’s and bank’s authorisation flows. Finops does not require the owner to store their online-banking password in the application. Enable Banking and the bank operate under their own applicable notices and terms. See Enable Banking’s privacy notice.

Storage and access

Retrieved records and reports are intended to be stored on the owner’s computer. API private keys and session credentials must be kept private and excluded from public repositories. Financial records are not published on this policy website, sold, or used for advertising. If the owner separately shares records with an AI service, backup service or another tool, that is an additional disclosure governed by that service’s terms and privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local history is retained until the owner deletes it. The owner controls local files and any backups. Revoking bank consent stops future authorised retrieval but does not automatically delete previously downloaded records or backups. Data-sharing consent can also be reviewed through Enable Banking’s consent portal.

This policy website

These static pages contain no application login, bank connection, analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process visitor technical information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about these pages, use the policy repository’s issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include bank data, private keys or other confidential information. The data-protection email supplied in the Enable Banking application is the private contact channel for that application.
